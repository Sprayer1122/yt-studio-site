Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

yt-studio uploader is a private tool used only by its owner. It connects to the owner's own YouTube channel through YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their details and schedule, and read the channel's statistics.

Data the tool accesses

Only the owner's own YouTube channel data: video uploads, video details and channel and video statistics. It does not collect, store or share personal data about any other person.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and channel statistics are stored on the owner's private server and are not shared with or sold to anyone. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Third-party services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
